Questions, Answered

Everything you're wondering.

Do fibroids really shrink naturally?
Fibroids are fed by hormone imbalance and inflammation. Our protocol corrects both, so growth slows, pressure eases and — as many of our patients' follow-up scans show — sizes can reduce substantially. We always advise honest monitoring with your own doctor.
I've been told surgery is my only option. What do I do?
Surgery is a real option for some cases, and you must never ignore your doctor's advice. What we offer is the chance to prepare the terrain naturally first — many patients reach a checkup where the plan changes, or a healthier state going into any procedure.
Can I still conceive with fibroids?
Many of our fibroid patients have gone on to conceive naturally. The protocol's aim is a womb that is calm, well-circulated and hormonally quiet — exactly the environment conception needs. We walk with you and your doctor at every stage.
I live far away. Can I still be treated?
Yes — most fibroid patients are guided remotely: consultation on WhatsApp video, remedies couriered nationwide, weekly check-ins and scan reviews with your local doctor. Distance has not stopped us yet.
How fast will I feel a difference?
Most women notice lighter, calmer cycles and reduced pelvic pressure within the first weeks, with deeper changes showing over three to six months — which is why our checkpoints are built around your scan dates.
