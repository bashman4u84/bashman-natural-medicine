His Story

A calling before it was
ever a practice.

As a young man, Bashman watched relatives shuttle between hospitals for years while their sicknesses only deepened. Searching for answers, he turned to the healing wisdom of the Prophet ﷺ — black seed, honey, olive oil, cupping — and studied it under scholars and master herbalists until people he prayed with began to recover.

What began as service to neighbours grew into a discipline. Today his protocols carry that double signature: prophetic in origin, proven in practice.
